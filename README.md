# SWAP Campus Nexus

Create a complete, production-quality, premium landing page for a student-focused digital platform called “SWAP”.

SWAP stands for:

BUY. SELL. REPEAT.

Use the provided SWAP logo as the official brand identity and do not redesign, distort, recolor, crop, or replace the logo. Preserve its visual character.

IMPORTANT BRAND POSITIONING

SWAP is NOT primarily a generic social network and NOT simply a product-sharing/swap platform.

SWAP is a CAMPUS-CENTRIC SECOND-HAND MARKETPLACE designed specifically for college students.

Its primary purpose is to make student-to-student buying and selling safer, more structured, private, and trustworthy than informal WhatsApp groups, messaging groups, notice boards, or direct peer-to-peer transactions.

Core positioning:

“Buy and sell second-hand products within your campus community — with privacy, structured listings, platform-mediated payments, and verified handovers.”

The product should feel like a combination of:

- A modern student marketplace

- A trusted campus ecosystem

- A privacy-first commerce platform

- A student opportunity hub

The landing page must immediately communicate:

1. Buy used products

2. Sell products and earn

3. Discover student services

4. Find student accommodation

5. Recover lost belongings

6. Complete transactions through a safer, structured process

SOURCE-OF-TRUTH PRODUCT FEATURES

The research-backed core SWAP marketplace includes:

- Institutional/college-based registration

- Student-focused second-hand marketplace

- Product listing and review

PLATFORM

      ↓

Around the platform create three highlighted trust mechanisms:

01

ANONYMOUS IDENTITY

Buyer and seller identities remain hidden from one another during the initial marketplace and purchasing stages.

02

PLATFORM-MEDIATED PAYMENT

The buyer pays through the platform flow rather than directly handing money to the seller. The transaction amount is controlled until the required completion step.

03

QR / CODE HANDOVER VERIFICATION

After payment confirmation, the buyer receives a unique QR or numeric verification code. During physical handover, the seller verifies the code. Successful verification marks the handover and allows the transaction to proceed to completion.
